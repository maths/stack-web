# Supporting STEM Teaching through Digital Assessment Practices, 5–9 October 2026

TTK University of Applied Sciences, Tallinn, Estonia

This Erasmus+ Blended Intensive Programme brings together STEM educators to explore meaningful digital assessment practices using STACK in Moodle. The programme combines hands-on STACK question authoring with discussions and practical sessions on AI-aware assessment design, learning analytics, research with STACK, JSXGraph integration, and advanced STACK tools. The aim is to equip STEM educators with practical skills to design digital assessments that evaluate mathematical reasoning and remain meaningful in the age of AI.

More information is available on our BIP webpage: [https://sites.google.com/tktk.ee/bipttk/Supporting-STEM-Teaching-through-Digital-Assessment-Practices](https://sites.google.com/tktk.ee/bipttk/Supporting-STEM-Teaching-through-Digital-Assessment-Practices)
